A fast-paced western arcade shooter on Roblox. I handled everything to do with the in-game world — levels, lobbies, incorporating new assets, and managing existing ones — with a lot of creative freedom to theme and build each map.

A month after release I was also brought on as a programmer, working on bugfixes, gun skins, and features like spectating, VIP server support, and daily rewards, and rewriting the capture-the-flag gamemode from the ground up. It taught me a lot about working in an existing codebase, and about reining in perfectionist tendencies when updates need to ship.

Roblox still occasionally tweets about Wild Revolvers to their followers — a nice sign that it remains a staple arcade shooter on the platform.
