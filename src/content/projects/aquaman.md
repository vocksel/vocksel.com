In July of 2018 I was subcontracted by [Roblox](https://corp.roblox.com) to work alongside others in developing an Aquaman-branded game. It was part of a sponsorship from Warner Bros. to create a game released in the weeks before the premiere of [Aquaman (2018)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1477834).

Over its lifetime the game received **8.7 million individual play sessions**, and was publicized by Roblox — on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UQZE-3t0wcM) and [Twitter](https://twitter.com/Roblox/status/1065024190875488256) — as well as the official [Aquaman movie Twitter account](https://twitter.com/aquamanmovie/status/1066390996915695616).
