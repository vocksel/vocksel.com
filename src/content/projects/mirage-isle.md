I used Mirage Isle as an "in between" project, adding to it whenever I needed a break from more intensive work. Modeling the rocks for the terrain was surprisingly therapeutic — it didn't take much thought to sculpt one that fit nicely into the landscape. A delightfully relaxing project, with a very appealing result.

Amidst the sea in a location seldom traveled rests Mirage Isle: a small landmass, sparsely covered with grass and trees. What appears to be a bastion of hope for weary travelers soon reveals itself as little more than a desert at sea — with no food or drinkable water, sailors have described it as a mirage.
