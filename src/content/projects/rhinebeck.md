My projects before Rhinebeck had only involved light 3D modeling, so I wanted to challenge myself and make the apartment something special. I spent a little over four months creating 50+ models to furnish the space. Some are large and easy to spot; others you might never notice — but they all contribute to the lived-in feeling I wanted to convey.

I love games where detail is packed into every nook and cranny, where there's so much to see you couldn't catch it all in a single playthrough. That desire to create something intricate and sentimental drove the whole build.
