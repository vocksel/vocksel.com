Fireflies is a small application that displays a quote and background image at random every time the page is loaded.

It started as an [AngularJS](https://angularjs.org) app, which fit my needs at the start, but adding new images and quotes was cumbersome. I later ported it to [Django](https://www.djangoproject.com), so now I can add a new image or quote directly from the admin interface.

All of the backgrounds and quotes mean a lot to me — I picked ones I personally liked — so I hold Fireflies very near to my heart.
